Privacy policy

Video Split by Radhe Rocky Star

Summary

Video Split collects only what it needs to run your account: your email address and a secure account ID. Your videos are processed only on your phone and are never uploaded to us or to anyone else. There are no ads and no tracking.

Your account

To use the app you create an account with your email address and a password. Accounts are provided by Firebase Authentication, a service of Google LLC. We store your email address and an account ID. We cannot see your password; Firebase keeps it in protected form. Firebase may also process technical information such as your IP address and device details to keep sign in secure and to prevent abuse.

Why we need it

We use your email address only to sign you in, to send a password reset or verification email when you ask for it, to link your subscription to your account, to protect the subscription system from misuse, and to answer your support messages. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not sell it or share it with anyone for their own use.

What the app does with your videos

When you choose a video, the app reads it through Android's own file picker. Clips are made on your phone only when you tap download, are saved to your gallery in an album named Video Split, and the temporary working file is deleted straight away. We never receive your videos, your clips or any part of them.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your phone number, location, contacts, photos, videos or advertising ID. There is no advertising and no analytics in the app.

Payments

Subscriptions are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. When you buy a plan, your account ID is attached to the purchase so the plan belongs to your account. Google tells the app which plan is active so the right qualities can be unlocked. Google's own privacy policy applies to payments.

Information stored on your phone

The app remembers that you are signed in, which plan you have, which account it belongs to and when it was last confirmed. This stays on your phone and is removed when you log out or uninstall the app.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your email address and account ID for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time inside the app: open the menu, tap Delete account and confirm with your password. You can also ask us to delete it by writing to lifeboxapp.dev@gmail.com from your registered email, or by following the steps on our Delete account web page. When an account is deleted, your email address and account ID are removed from our sign in system. Deleting your account does not cancel your Google Play subscription, so please cancel it in Google Play first. Google may keep payment records as its own policies and the law require.

Permissions

Saving clips to your gallery may ask for storage or gallery permission on some phones. The app uses an internet connection to sign you in and to talk to Google Play about your subscription.

Third parties

The app uses two services from Google LLC: Firebase Authentication for sign in, and Google Play for subscriptions. Video processing is done on your phone by FFmpeg and does not use the internet.

Children

Video Split is not made for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you think a child has created an account, write to us and we will delete it.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you at any time. You can delete any clip from your gallery, log out, delete your account or remove the app whenever you like. To ask a question about your privacy, write to lifeboxapp.dev@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date below. Last updated: October 2026.
