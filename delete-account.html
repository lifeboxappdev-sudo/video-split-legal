Delete your account

You can delete your Video Split account and the data linked to it in either of two ways.

Option 1: inside the app

Open Video Split, tap the menu, tap Delete account, enter your password and confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

Option 2: by email

If you cannot open the app, write to lifeboxapp.dev@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with. Use the subject Delete my account. We will delete your account within 7 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

Your email address and account ID are removed from our sign in system. We never receive your videos, so there are no videos to delete. Google may keep payment records as its own policies and the law require.

Your subscription

Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription. Before deleting, cancel it in the Google Play Store: tap your profile picture, then Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions. If you delete your account first, you can still cancel there at any time. No refund is given only because an account was deleted; see our Terms and conditions.
